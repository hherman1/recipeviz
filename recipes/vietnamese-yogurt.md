---
title: Vietnamese Yogurt
description: Sua chua: condensed milk yogurt set in small jars in a cooling oven.
tags: yogurt, fermentation, make-ahead
---

```recipe
STARTER: 60-100 ml fresh yogurt starter, sweetened or unsweetened
bring fully to room temperature for 1-2 hours STARTER

JARS: 8 125 ml jars and utensils
sterilize in boiling water for 30 seconds JARS
dry at 100°C for 2-3 minutes or air-dry completely JARS

MILK: 840 ml unsweetened milk
COND: 160 ml sweetened condensed milk
BASE: stir together MILK COND
heat to 80-85°C, stirring continuously BASE
cool to 40-43°C BASE

CULTURED: gently temper starter with 2-3 tbsp milk, then fold into the remaining milk BASE STARTER
strain if needed CULTURED
FILLED: divide among jars, skim bubbles, and cover CULTURED JARS

OVEN: heat oven to 70-80°C for 5 minutes
INCUBATING: place jars in oven, then turn oven off FILLED OVEN
after 1-1.5 hours, heat oven to 50-60°C for 2-3 minutes
after another 1-1.5 hours, heat oven to 50-60°C for 1-2 minutes
incubate without opening the oven or moving the jars until set and tangy, 4-5 hours total
refrigerate when set and sufficiently tart
```

## Notes

Sweetened condensed milk is what separates this from plain yogurt — it sets
firmer and tastes closer to a custard than a tart breakfast yogurt.

Temperatures do most of the work:

- **80-85 C** for the milk. This is a denature-the-proteins step, not a boil.
  Stir continuously or the bottom scorches.
- **40-43 C** before the starter goes in. Hotter kills the culture; colder
  stalls it. Tempering the starter with a few tablespoons of warm milk first
  keeps it from seizing into lumps.
- **Around 45 C** during incubation. The oven is used as an insulated box, not
  as a heater: it is warmed, switched off, and nudged back up twice.

The two short reheats are the fussy part of the schedule. Set a timer for the
first at 1-1.5 hours and the second 1-1.5 hours after that, then leave the
jars completely alone until the 4-5 hour mark.

## Judging when it is done

Tilt a jar. Set yogurt pulls away from the glass in one piece and leaves a
clean edge; unset yogurt slides. Some whey on the surface is fine. Refrigerate
as soon as it is set and tart enough — the flavor keeps sharpening while it is
warm, and a jar left too long turns sour and weeps.
